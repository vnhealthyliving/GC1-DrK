Medicare & GLP-1

Does Medicare cover GLP-1 medications like Ozempic or Wegovy?+
Coverage depends on the specific medication and your diagnosis. Medicare Part D has historically covered GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic and Mounjaro when prescribed for Type 2 diabetes. Coverage for obesity-only indications is evolving under new CMS guidance. Dr. K will review your specific plan and diagnosis to determine what is covered. Call (619) 429-7700 to check your eligibility.
What is the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program?+
The Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program refers to newly expanded CMS pathways that allow Medicare to cover GLP-1 medications for qualifying patients as part of a comprehensive obesity and weight management benefit. At VN Healthy Living, Dr. K evaluates your eligibility and helps navigate the coverage process. Call (619) 429-7700 to learn more.

Who is eligible for Medicare-covered GLP-1 therapy?+
Medicare coverage for GLP-1 therapy is based on several clinical factors. High-level eligibility guidelines generally include:
  • BMI of 30 or higher (obesity), or
  • BMI of 27 or higher with at least one weight-related chronic condition — such as Type 2 diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, cardiovascular disease, or obstructive sleep apnea
  • Prior authorization and medical necessity — Dr. K must evaluate your individual case, document clinical justification, and submit the required paperwork to Medicare or your Medicare Advantage plan

Every patient's situation is unique. Coverage decisions depend on your specific health profile, active diagnoses, and plan requirements. A consultation with Dr. K is critical — he will assess your eligibility, build the clinical case, and navigate the process with you. Call (619) 429-7700 to schedule.
