Who is eligible for Medicare-covered GLP-1 therapy? +
Medicare coverage for GLP-1 therapy is based on several clinical factors. High-level eligibility guidelines generally include:
  • BMI of 30 or higher (obesity), or
  • BMI of 27 or higher with at least one weight-related chronic condition such as Type 2 diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, cardiovascular disease, or obstructive sleep apnea
  • Prior authorization and medical necessity documentation — Dr. K must evaluate your individual situation, complete the required clinical justification, and submit the necessary paperwork to Medicare or your Medicare Advantage plan

Every patient's case is unique. Coverage decisions depend on your specific health profile, active diagnoses, and plan requirements. A consultation with Dr. K is critical — he will assess your eligibility, build the clinical case, and guide you through the process. Call (619) 429-7700 to schedule.
