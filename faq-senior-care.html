Who is eligible for Senior Care covered weight management therapy?
Senior Care coverage for weight management therapy is based on several clinical factors. High-level eligibility guidelines generally include:
  • BMI of 30 or higher (obesity), or
  • BMI of 27 or higher with at least one weight-related chronic condition such as Type 2 diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, cardiovascular disease, or obstructive sleep apnea
  • Prior authorization and medical necessity documentation — Dr. K must evaluate your individual situation, complete the required clinical justification, and submit the necessary paperwork to your insurance

Every patient's case is unique. Coverage decisions depend on your specific health profile, active diagnoses, and plan requirements. A consultation with Dr. K is critical — he will assess your eligibility, build the clinical case, and guide you through the process. Call (619) 429-7700 to schedule.

What is the Senior Care Weight Management Program?
The Senior Care Weight Management Program is a CMS demonstration program (July 1, 2026 – December 31, 2027) that provides Senior Care Part D beneficiaries access to covered Weight Management weight management medications at a flat $50 monthly copay for qualifying chronic conditions including obesity, cardiovascular disease, and metabolic disorders. Dr. K at VN Healthy Living is fully enrolled to prescribe through this program and manages the complete prior authorization process on your behalf.
