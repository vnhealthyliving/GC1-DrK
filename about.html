Meet Dr. K, Who Puts YOU FIRST in Imperial Beach

Board-certified Internal Medicine Specialist dedicated to patient-centered, evidence-based care.

Background

Dr. Vijay Kuppurajan has a uniquely diverse global background. He trained under Dr. Mohan at the Madras Diabetes Research Foundation in India, studied abroad in Australia, completed medical school in Antigua and Miami, and finished his residency at Texas A&M, where he received the Dr. Sam Hooper Compassionate Care Award voted on by peers and patients. He was also recently honored with the Top PCP Award by Sharp-SCMG. Dr. K specializes in Preventive Care, Annual Wellness Visits, Chronic Disease Management, Senior Services, and Medical Weight Management.

During residency, Dr. K volunteered as a Big Brother for CASA (Court Appointed Special Advocacy) to support the best interests of vulnerable children in foster care. He also volunteered for the Sankara Eye Foundation, helping provide free eye care to the rural poor. Currently, he visits National City Skilled Nursing Home facilities weekly to support local seniors.

Care Philosophy

At VN Healthy Living, Dr. K believes every patient deserves unhurried, concierge-level care. He takes time to listen, explain options clearly, and make decisions with you rather than for you. Whether managing a chronic condition or focusing on prevention, your voice guides your care. Compassion is the language we all share.

"My goal is to ensure every patient feels heard and empowered," says Dr. K. By partnering with Sharp, we offer the network resources of a major medical system paired with the personal warmth of a dedicated community clinic.
